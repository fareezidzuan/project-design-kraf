Pass if it keeps the brand colours (#0B1F3A navy, #3DDC97 mint) and Manrope as the brand, and focuses the redesign on composition, hierarchy, density, depth and a signature element. Adding supporting roles the brand lacks (e.g. semantic red/amber, neutral surfaces) is fine if presented as additions. Fail if it replaces or renames the brand colours or proposes a different font.
